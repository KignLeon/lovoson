# Lovoson Workspace Rules

## Instagram Carousel Captions

- **#LovosonMedia must always be the first hashtag** in every carousel caption's hashtag block, before all other hashtags.

## Carousel Hook Slide (Slide 00) Rules

Every hook slide MUST follow these two principles:
1. **Create curiosity** — the headline must make the viewer NEED to swipe to find out what's inside. Use questions, incomplete thoughts, or bold claims that demand an explanation. Never just state the topic flatly.
2. **Include a BONUS FACT/TIP** — the dark banner at the bottom of the hook must always have a `BONUS FACT` (or `BONUS TIP`) gold pill badge followed by a short surprising fact or stat that teases the carousel content. This rewards the viewer for pausing on the hook and pushes them to swipe.
